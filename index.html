成语日历 Chengyu Calendar

One Chinese idiom a day, on a tear-off calendar for the iPhone, with its pinyin, a gloss of each character, its meaning, an example, the story of where it comes from, and three sentences that use it, in Simplified or Traditional characters. A Home Screen widget shows the day's page.

Chengyu Calendar Privacy Policy

Operator: Zhiyin ("we", "our"), Ireland

App: Chengyu Calendar, for iPhone, and its Home Screen widget

Contact: fiki@zhiyin.dev

Effective date: from the first release of Chengyu Calendar on the App Store, September 2026

Chengyu Calendar shows one Chinese idiom a day on a tear-off calendar. It is built to need as little of your data as possible. This policy says exactly what the app does with data, which is almost nothing.

1. What we collect

Nothing. Chengyu Calendar has no accounts and no sign-in. It does not send anything about you to us, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code of any kind. We never see who uses the app.

2. What stays on your device

The app keeps a few things on your phone only, in the app's own storage, and they never leave it:

  • Your choice of script. Whether the pad is printed in Simplified or Traditional characters.
  • The widget's pages. The app prints the next two weeks of calendar pages as images so the Home Screen widget can show them. They are stored in the app's shared container on your phone.
  • Motion. The light on the calendar's board and brass moves as you tilt the phone. To do this the app reads the phone's motion sensors while it is open. The readings are used on the spot to place a reflection and are not stored, logged or transmitted.

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

3. Purchases

This section applies once Chengyu Calendar offers a subscription or a one-time purchase. If the version you have offers none, no purchase data exists.

Purchases are made through Apple's App Store. Apple handles your payment details; we never receive your card or bank information. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in Apple's own privacy policy.

To know whether your purchase is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a service that checks purchase receipts with Apple. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier generated for your copy of the app, the receipt of the purchase, and basic device information such as the app version and the device type. It does not receive your name, email or Apple ID. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy. You can ask us to have this anonymous purchase record deleted by writing to the address above.

4. Writing to us

If you tap Contact on the back of the calendar, the app opens your mail app with a message addressed to us. The message is pre-filled with the app's version, the iOS version and your device model, so that we can help you. You see all of this before you send it, and you can delete any of it. We receive only what you choose to send.

5. Diagnostics

If you have chosen in your iPhone's settings to share analytics and crash reports with app developers, Apple may send us anonymised crash reports for Chengyu Calendar. These contain no personal information. This is controlled entirely by your iPhone's settings under Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

6. Third parties

The only third parties involved are Apple, which distributes the app, handles purchases and may pass on anonymised diagnostics, and, once purchases are offered, RevenueCat, as described above. We sell no data, share no data for advertising, and have nothing else to share.

7. Children

Chengyu Calendar is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal data, it collects none from children.

8. Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or export. Everything the app keeps is on your phone and is yours to delete by deleting the app. Purchase records are held by Apple under your Apple ID and by RevenueCat under an anonymous identifier; write to us to have the RevenueCat record deleted. If you are in the European Union you may also raise a concern with your local data protection authority.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be shipped with the app and the effective date above updated. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the new policy.

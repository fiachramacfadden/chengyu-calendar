2What the app is

Chengyu Calendar is for people learning Chinese. Every day it shows a new Chinese idiom, known as a chengyu, with its meaning, its story and examples of how it is used, in Simplified or Traditional characters. Its design is inspired by the Chinese tear-off calendar, and it is made to live on your Home Screen as a widget. You don’t need an account, and you only need an internet connection to buy, restore or check your subscription.

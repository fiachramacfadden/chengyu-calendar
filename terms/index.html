2. What the app is

Chengyu Calendar is a tear-off calendar for the iPhone. Each day it shows one Chinese idiom (成语) with its pinyin, a gloss of each character, its meaning, an example, the story of where it comes from, and three sentences that use it, in Simplified or Traditional characters as you choose. A Home Screen widget shows the day's page. The app works without an account, without an internet connection, and without collecting anything about you (see the Privacy Policy).
